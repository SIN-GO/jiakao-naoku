# 安全、隐私与权利报告 / Security, privacy and rights reports

不要在公开Issue、提交、截图或学习记录备份中上传账号令牌、密码、身份证号、私人联系方式或他人的个人信息。
Do not post tokens, passwords, government IDs, private contact details or other people's personal information in public issues, commits, screenshots or progress backups.

安全问题请优先使用仓库Security页的“Report a vulnerability”（如果已启用）；未启用时只提不含利用细节和秘密的联系请求。
For security concerns, prefer “Report a vulnerability” in the repository Security tab when available; otherwise post only a non-sensitive contact request.

权利问题请说明涉及的文件/题目ID、权利依据和期望处理方式；请勿公开敏感证明文件。
For rights concerns, identify the file/question ID, basis of the claim and requested action; do not post sensitive proof publicly.
维护者应核实后更正或撤下内容；本说明不表示已启用任何特定GitHub功能，也不承诺处理时限。
Maintainers should investigate and correct or remove affected content. This guide does not imply a particular GitHub reporting feature is enabled or promise a response time.
